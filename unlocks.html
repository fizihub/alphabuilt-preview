Good to know

How much does it cost?

There is no price list. The car, the parts and the goal change the job, so we quote each one. Send the year, model and what you want, and you get a price and a date back.

Why does my 2020+ BMW need an unlock first?

Newer BMW and Supra ECUs leave the factory locked, so they cannot be tuned through the port. We remove the ECU, unlock it (bench or Femto, depending on the car) and re-install it.

Does the unlock tune the car?

No. The unlock opens the ECU so it can accept a tune. After that you choose any tuning platform, or we write a custom tune for the car.

I'm not in South Florida. Can you still tune my car?

Yes. Custom tunes can be done remotely. For unlocks and installs, DM us and we explain how it works for your car.

Do you only do performance work?

No. Oil changes and routine service are part of the shop too, so the car can stay with people who already know it.
